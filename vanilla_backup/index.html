Character Profile ✦ Track 1

Small skull,
big dreams.

Pip is a sleepy, gentle skull baby with huge soft eye sockets, a tiny crooked smile, and an oversized starry nightcap with a pink bow. Pip never frightens — only soothes.

As Junior Dream Engineer, Pip personally oversees the lullabies, glowing nightlights, and bedtime stories that help the neighbourhood's little creatures sleep. Every night, Pip floats across rooftops delivering custom-made dreams in tiny woven pouches. 🌙

Chapter 1 · Before the Dreams

The Quiet Before

Long before Pip arrived, the neighbourhood's nights were restless. Little creatures tossed and turned. Stars seemed too far away. The moon watched — and worried. Something was missing. Someone was needed.

✨
Chapter 2 · The Birth of Pip

Born from Moonlight

On the quietest night of the year, the moon shed a single silver tear. It fell into a tiny cradle of clouds — and from it emerged Pip. Smooth skull, amber-glowing eyes, a starry nightcap already perfectly perched. Ready for work.

🏮
Chapter 3 · First Day at Work

The Midnight Nursery Opens

Pip was hired immediately as Junior Dream Engineer. The Midnight Nursery — a cozy workshop at the top of the tallest cloud — became home. Pip learned to weave lullabies, wire lanterns, and fold dreams into neat little parcels.
